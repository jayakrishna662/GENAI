COMPANY POLICY DOCUMENT

1. ANNUAL LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Employees can carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days
from one calendar year to the next.

Employees must submit annual leave requests through the HR portal
at least 2 working days before the requested leave date.

Employees should contact their manager if they need emergency leave
that could not be requested in advance.


2. SICK LEAVE POLICY

Employees are entitled to 12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

For sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive working days,
employees may be required to provide a medical certificate.

Sick leave should be reported to the employee's manager as soon
as possible.


3. WORK FROM HOME POLICY

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week.

Employees must submit their work-from-home request at least
one working day in advance.

Managers may reject a work-from-home request if business
requirements require the employee to work from the office.


4. ATTENDANCE POLICY

Employees are expected to work 8 hours per working day.

The standard working hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
including a one-hour lunch break.

Employees who expect to arrive late should inform their manager
as early as possible.


5. PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY

Employees receive formal performance reviews twice a year.

The standard review periods are June and December.

Performance reviews consider work quality, productivity,
teamwork, communication, and achievement of assigned goals.


6. PROMOTION POLICY

Employees may be considered for promotion based on their
performance, experience, skills, and business requirements.

A strong performance review does not automatically guarantee
a promotion.

Promotion decisions are made by the employee's manager together
with the relevant department leadership.


7. TRAVEL POLICY

Employees must obtain manager approval before booking
business travel.

Business travel expenses must be submitted through the
company expense system within 15 days after returning from travel.

Employees should choose reasonably priced transportation
and accommodation options.


8. EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

Eligible full-time employees receive health insurance coverage.

Employees may also receive additional benefits depending on
their role, employment level, and company policies.

Benefits may be reviewed and changed by the company from time
to time.


9. TRAINING AND LEARNING

Employees are encouraged to participate in professional
training and learning programs.

Employees may request approval for training courses that are
relevant to their current role or career development.

The company may cover approved training expenses according
to its learning and development policy.
